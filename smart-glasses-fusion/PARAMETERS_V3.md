# Smart Glasses V3 - Rounded Half Frame

V3 is a smart-glasses carrier rather than a prescription frame. It retains only two upper brow
beams, the center bridge and side connection blocks. There are no lower lens rims.

## Ergonomic changes

| Feature | V3 design |
|---|---|
| Front structure | Two upper brow beams plus center bridge |
| Lower frame | Removed |
| Brow beam edge radius | 1.8 mm nominal |
| Temple edge radius | 2.0 mm nominal |
| Electronics housing edge radius | 0.7 mm where wall geometry permits |
| Ear hook | Continuous swept curve, 27 mm downward drop |
| Electronics | Camera, controller, battery, switch, amplifier and speaker all on right |

The adult head and 4 mm comfort keepout remain visible in the assembly STEP. They are reference
bodies and must not be manufactured.

## Fusion 360 files

- `output/smart_glasses_v3_half_frame_assembly.step`: full review assembly with head references.
- `output/smart_glasses_v3_half_frame_shell.step`: printable concept shells only.
- `output/smart_glasses_v3_half_frame_shell.stl`: printable concept mesh.
- `output/smart_glasses_v3_half_frame.FCStd`: generated source model.

Before a wearable prototype, measure the actual wearer's head width, brow-to-ear height,
temple-to-ear distance and ear-back clearance.
